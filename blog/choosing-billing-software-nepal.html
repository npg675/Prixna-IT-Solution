How to choose billing software for your shop in Nepal

📅 October 2026⏱ 6 min read🏷 Buying guide

Every week, a shop owner in Kathmandu asks us the same question: "Which billing software is best?" The honest answer: the best software is the one your staff will actually use every day — with correct VAT/PAN handling, stock that matches the shelf, and support that answers the phone.

1. Start with your tax reality

If you issue VAT bills, your software must print IRD-compatible invoices with your PAN/VAT number, sequential numbering, and correct tax breakup. Ask the vendor to show you a printed sample — not a slideshow. If you mostly sell retail without VAT bills, a simpler, faster billing flow matters more than tax features you'll never open.

2. Demand these five features

  • Fast counter billing — barcode search, hold/recall bills, and credit ("udharo") tracking.
  • Honest inventory — purchase entry, stock deduction per sale, and low-stock alerts.
  • Daily closing report — cash vs. digital vs. credit, in one screen, every evening.
  • Offline-first working — internet in Nepal drops; billing must not stop when it does.
  • Backup you control — daily automatic backup, plus a copy you can take off-site.

3. Ask these questions in the demo

  • "Show me a full sale, return, and day-closing — timed, without rehearsal."
  • "What happens when the internet is off for a day?"
  • "Who trains my staff, and what does a support visit cost after year one?"
  • "How do I get my data out if I ever switch systems?"

4. The free-first rule

Before paying for anything, check whether free tools already cover part of the job — our own JigriTools suite handles quotations, EMI math, and document utilities at zero cost. Spend software money where it removes daily pain: the billing counter.
