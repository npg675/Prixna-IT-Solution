Stop registering attendance on paper

📅 October 2026⏱ 5 min read🏷 Productivity

Paper registers lie — buddy-punching, torn pages, and month-end arguments over "I came at 9:45, not 10:15." A fingerprint device ends the debate for less than most businesses spend on tea in a month. Here's how to buy right.

What a basic setup gives you

  • Trustworthy in/out times per employee, per day — no handwriting disputes.
  • Late/early/overtime summaries for salary calculation week.
  • Leave records alongside attendance, so balances are visible — not remembered.
  • Multi-device support if you later add a second branch or door access.

What to ask before you buy

  • "Is the attendance software included, and does it run without internet?"
  • "Can it export a monthly salary-ready report (Excel) my accountant accepts?"
  • "Who enrolls fingerprints and trains my manager — and what if a fingerprint doesn't read well?" (Face/card options exist for such cases.)
  • "What is the exact warranty and what does a service visit cost?"
