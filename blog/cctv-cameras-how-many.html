CCTV for shops: how many cameras do you really need?

📅 October 2026⏱ 5 min read🏷 Security

Most small shops in Kathmandu are fully covered with 4 cameras — entrance, cash counter, main sales floor, and godown/back door. Offices usually need 4–8 depending on layout. Anything beyond that should be justified by a walk-through, not a price list.

The 4 positions that matter

  • Entrance (face height): mounted to capture faces coming in — not just the tops of heads.
  • Cash counter: tight view of billing and cash handling; your highest-value angle.
  • Sales floor / shelves: wide view of customer movement and blind corners.
  • Godown / back exit: where stock quietly walks away.

Storage math in 30 seconds

A 1TB hard drive typically stores roughly 2–4 weeks of 4-camera HD footage (motion recording stretches this further). Decide upfront how many days of backup you need — for dispute resolution, 15–30 days is the common ask — and size the drive accordingly.

Three installation mistakes to avoid

  • Cameras too high: a camera at 12 feet films scalps. Faces need 7–9 feet at entry points.
  • No power backup: loadshedding-era habit — put the DVR and key cameras on a UPS so recording survives outages.
  • No remote-view test: insist the installer configures phone viewing and tests it on your phone before leaving.
