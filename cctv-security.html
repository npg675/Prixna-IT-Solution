Common problems

Why shop CCTV often disappoints

Most complaints we hear trace back to rushed planning — not the cameras themselves.

🕳️

Blind spots

Cameras pointed at walls instead of the counter, entrance and stock areas where incidents actually happen.

🌙

Useless night footage

Wrong camera type or position leaves faces unrecognisable after dark, exactly when you need clarity.

💾

Recording runs out

An undersized hard disk overwrites footage in days. Storage should be sized to your camera count and needs.

🔌

Messy, fragile cabling

Loose wires and unprotected joints fail early and look unprofessional. Neat, labelled work lasts longer.
