Privacy policy

Short version: we use your details only to respond to your enquiry. We never sell data or send bulk spam.

What we collect

When you submit a form, we collect the details you enter: name, phone, email, company, location, service interest and message — plus technical source data (campaign parameters, referrer) used to understand which pages generate enquiries.

How we use it

  • To call, message or email you back about your enquiry.
  • To prepare quotations, demos or site visits you requested.
  • To send follow-up reminders about your open enquiry, where you consented.

What we never do

  • No selling or renting your details to anyone.
  • No unsolicited bulk SMS, calls or messaging.
  • No publishing your information on the website without written permission (testimonials and case studies are published only with client approval).

Storage & your rights

Currently enquiries are stored in a device-local lead inbox until a server/CRM integration is configured (see js/config.js). You may ask at any time — by phone +977 9813714582 or email admin@prixnaitsolution.com.np — to view, correct or delete your details, and we will comply promptly.
