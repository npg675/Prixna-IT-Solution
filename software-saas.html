Which business software do I actually need?
It depends on your daily work. Shops usually start with billing and inventory, restaurants with KOT billing, and growing firms with ERP or CRM. A free consultation helps you choose without overspending.
Do you provide setup and training?
Yes. We install and configure the software, migrate your basic records where possible, and train your staff practically until they can run daily work confidently.

Yes. We recommend and configure systems that support VAT/PAN invoices, day-book records and the reports your accountant needs at month-end.

Every business needs a different setup, so pricing is quoted in NPR after a free consultation. You receive an itemised quotation with no hidden charges.

Yes. Phone, WhatsApp and on-site support are available under agreed terms, with annual maintenance options for businesses that want ongoing care.
