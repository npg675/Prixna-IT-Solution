Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which biometric device suits my office?
It depends on staff count, shifts and whether you need door access too. We recommend fingerprint, card or face options after understanding your attendance rules.
Can attendance connect to payroll?
Yes. We set up monthly late, overtime and leave summaries that your accountant can use directly, and explain how the reports map to your salary process.
Do you train our staff to use the system?
Yes. We enrol fingerprints, configure shifts and train your admin person on reports, corrections and new-staff onboarding.
What happens during power cuts?
Devices keep stored records without power, and we advise on small UPS backup so check-in continues during load-shedding or outages.
How much does a biometric system cost?
Cost depends on device type, staff capacity and door-access hardware. After a free consultation you receive a transparent itemised NPR quotation.
