Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much will my repair cost?
Every fault is different, so we diagnose first and give you a transparent NPR quote before any repair work. You approve the price — no work starts without your go-ahead.
Will I lose my files during repair?
We take data safety seriously and back up where possible before invasive work. Still, keeping your own backup is always wisest — ask us to set one up.
How long does a typical repair take?
Software issues are often same-day; hardware depending on parts availability takes longer. We tell you an honest timeframe at diagnosis and update you if it changes.
Do you offer annual maintenance for offices?
Yes. Ask for our AMC terms — scheduled check-ups, priority breakdown visits and agreed response times for your computers, printers and network.
Do you repair printers and other equipment?
Yes, we service printers, desktops, laptops and related peripherals. Bring the device to our Koteshwor shop or ask about on-site visits for offices.
