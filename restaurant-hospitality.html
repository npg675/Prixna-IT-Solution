FAQs

Restaurant software questions, answered

What is KOT billing and do I need it?
KOT (Kitchen Order Ticket) sends table orders straight to the kitchen printer or screen, reducing mistakes and delays. Any dine-in outlet with a separate kitchen benefits from it.
Do you also supply the hardware?
Yes. We supply and install billing terminals, thermal printers, kitchen printers and networking, then configure the software to work with them as one system.
Can the system handle split bills and table transfers?
Yes. Table-wise orders, merging and splitting bills, discounts and day-end settlement are standard features we configure for your outlet.
How much does restaurant software cost in Nepal?
Outlets differ in tables, printers and branches, so pricing is quoted in NPR after a free consultation. You receive an itemised quotation first.
Do you train our counter and kitchen staff?
Yes. Practical training for billing staff and kitchen workflow is included, plus support under agreed terms after opening.
