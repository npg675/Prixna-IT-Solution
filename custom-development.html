FAQs

Custom project questions, answered

Should I buy ready-made software or build custom?
If ready-made software covers most of your workflow, buy it — it is cheaper and faster. Build custom only when your process is genuinely unique. We advise honestly either way.
What does a business website cost in Nepal?
Every project differs, so we quote in NPR after a free consultation based on pages, features and content readiness. You pay for what you need, nothing more.
How long does a project take?
A simple business website typically takes a few weeks once content is ready. Custom systems are scheduled in clear phases with milestones you can review.
Will I own my website and domain?
Yes. Domains are registered in your name, and you receive admin access plus practical training to manage everyday updates yourself.
Do you maintain websites after launch?
Yes. Updates, backups, small changes and technical support are available under agreed maintenance terms.
