Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why is our office Wi-Fi slow in some rooms?
Usually one router is trying to cover too large or walled-up an area. We check placement, interference and load, then add access points where the signal actually drops.
Should our office use wired LAN or Wi-Fi?
Fixed desks, billing counters and printers work best wired; laptops and guests use Wi-Fi. Most reliable offices run both, with structured cabling at the core.
How do we stay online during power cuts?
We put your router, switch and fibre ONT on a small UPS so internet survives short outages, and size backup for your actual equipment load.
Do small offices need a server?
Not always. If you share files, run billing software centrally or need controlled backups, a small server or NAS pays off — otherwise a well-organised network plus cloud backup may be enough.
How much does office networking cost?
It depends on points, cable runs and equipment. After a free consultation and Valley site visit you receive a transparent itemised NPR quotation.
